This document is a demonstration standard. It defines security requirements, testing procedures and guidance for any organisation that stores, processes or transmits member data. It is published in HTML, PDF, OSCAL and spreadsheet form from a single source.

1. 適用範囲

This standard specifies requirements for protecting member data throughout its life cycle. It applies to every entity that stores, processes or transmits member data, and to every system component included in, or connected to, the member data environment.

This standard does not specify how an entity organises its staff, and it does not replace legal or contractual obligations that apply to the entity.

Each requirement in clauses 4 to 6 consists of four parts:

  • a stable identifier, which matches the clause number;

  • the requirement statement, which is normative;

  • one or more testing procedures, which an assessor follows to confirm that the requirement is met;

  • guidance, which is informative.

1.1. Requirement language

In this standard the word "shall" indicates a requirement, the word "should" indicates a recommendation, and the word "may" indicates a permission. These words carry that meaning only in normative clauses. Guidance, notes, the annex and the bibliography are informative.

2. 引用規格

The following documents are referred to in the text in such a way that some or all of their content constitutes requirements of this standard.

  • IETF RFC 8446, The Transport Layer Security (TLS) Protocol Version 1.3. Available at https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8446.

  • IETF RFC 3339, Date and Time on the Internet: Timestamps. Available at https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3339.

3. 用語及び定義

この基準で用いる主な用語及び定義は、次による。

assessor

person or organisation that evaluates whether an entity meets the requirements of this standard

entity

organisation that stores, processes or transmits member data

member data

information that identifies a member, together with any account or transaction information linked to that member

member data environment

people, processes and system components that store, process or transmit member data

privileged account

account with permissions beyond those of a standard user, such as the ability to change security settings or read all member data

strong cryptography

cryptography based on publicly reviewed algorithms with an effective key strength of at least 128 bits

system component

network device, server, computing device, virtual component or application included in or connected to the member data environment

user identifier

value that uniquely identifies one person or one automated process to a system component

4. アクセス管理

Access to member data is limited to people and processes that need it, and every action can be traced to the person or process that performed it.

4.1. Identification

4.1.1. Unique user identifiers

The entity shall assign a unique user identifier to each person and each automated process before allowing access to any system component.

テスト手順
4.1.1.a

Examine the account management procedure to verify that it requires a unique user identifier for each person and each automated process.

4.1.1.b

Examine the account list of a sample of system components to verify that no user identifier is assigned to more than one person.

ガイダンス

When several people share one identifier, actions cannot be traced to an individual. Service accounts used by software are in scope: each one needs its own identifier and a named owner.

要求事項へのコメント 4.1.1

4.1.2. Removal of access on departure

事業体は、要員が事業体を離れてから1営業日以内に、その要員の利用者識別子を無効にしなければならない。

テスト手順
4.1.2.a

Examine the leaver procedure to verify that it defines who disables user identifiers and within what time.

4.1.2.b

Examine records for a sample of people who left in the last six months to verify that their user identifiers were disabled within one business day.

ガイダンス

Accounts that remain active after a person leaves are a common route for unauthorised access. Linking the leaver procedure to the human resources system reduces the delay.

要求事項へのコメント 4.1.2

4.2. Access review

4.2.1. Periodic review of access rights

The entity shall review access rights at least as often as 表 1 specifies, and shall remove any access that is no longer needed.

The entity should record the outcome of each review, including the name of the reviewer.

テスト手順
4.2.1.a

Examine review records to verify that reviews took place at the frequency that 表 1 specifies.

4.2.1.b

Interview the people responsible to verify that access identified as no longer needed was removed.

4.2.1.c

Examine a sample of privileged accounts to verify that each one has a documented business reason.

ガイダンス

Access tends to accumulate as people change roles. A review is most effective when the person who performs it understands what each role needs.

要求事項へのコメント 4.2.1

5. データ保護

Member data is unreadable to anyone who obtains it without authorisation, whether it is stored or in transit.

5.1. Storage

5.1.1. Encryption of stored member data

The entity shall render stored member data unreadable by using strong cryptography.

The entity shall store cryptographic keys separately from the data that they protect.

テスト手順
5.1.1.a

Examine system configurations to verify that stored member data is encrypted with strong cryptography.

5.1.1.b

Examine key storage locations to verify that keys are not stored with the data that they protect.

ガイダンス

Encryption protects data only while the keys stay secret. A key stored beside the data that it protects gives little protection if the storage is copied.[1]

要求事項へのコメント 5.1.1

5.1.2. Retention limits

The entity shall define a retention period for each category of member data, and shall securely delete member data when its retention period ends.

テスト手順
5.1.2.a

Examine the retention policy to verify that it defines a retention period for each category of member data.

5.1.2.b

Examine a sample of data stores to verify that they hold no member data older than the defined retention period.

ガイダンス

Data that has been deleted cannot be stolen. Automated deletion jobs are more reliable than manual clean-up.

要求事項へのコメント 5.1.2

5.2. Transmission

5.2.1. Encryption in transit

The entity shall protect member data sent over any network outside its control by using Transport Layer Security version 1.3, as specified in IETF RFC 8446, or a protocol of equivalent strength.

The entity may use an earlier protocol version on an internal network segment that carries no member data.

テスト手順
5.2.1.a

Examine the configuration of a sample of external connections to verify that they accept only the protocol versions that the requirement allows.

5.2.1.b

Observe a sample of transmissions to verify that member data is encrypted in transit.

ガイダンス

Networks outside the control of the entity include the internet, wireless networks and links operated by third parties. 図 1 shows where encryption applies.

要求事項へのコメント 5.2.1

6. ログ記録及び監視

Activity that affects member data is recorded, kept and reviewed so that misuse can be detected and investigated.

6.1. Audit logs

6.1.1. Recording of access to member data

The entity shall record every access to member data in an audit log. Each log entry shall include:

  • the user identifier;

  • the date and time, in the format that IETF RFC 3339 specifies;

  • the action performed;

  • whether the action succeeded.

テスト手順
6.1.1.a

Examine logging configurations to verify that access to member data is recorded.

6.1.1.b

Examine a sample of log entries to verify that each one includes all the listed fields.

ガイダンス

A log that omits the user identifier cannot support an investigation. A single time format across all system components makes it possible to place events in order.

要求事項へのコメント 6.1.1

6.1.2. Retention and review of audit logs

The entity shall keep audit logs for at least 12 months, and shall review them for unusual activity at least once each week.

テスト手順
6.1.2.a

Examine log storage to verify that audit logs from the last 12 months are available.

6.1.2.b

Examine review records to verify that reviews took place at least once each week.

6.1.2.c

Interview the people responsible to verify that they follow up on unusual activity.

ガイダンス

Many incidents are discovered months after they begin. Automated alerting can support the weekly review but does not replace it.
