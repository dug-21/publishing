[[access-management]]
== Access management

Access to member data is limited to people and processes that need it, and every action can be traced to the person or process that performed it.

[[identification]]
=== Identification

[[REQ-4.1.1]]
[.requirement]
==== Unique user identifiers

[.statement]
--
The entity shall assign a unique <<term-user-identifier,user identifier>> to each person and each automated process before allowing access to any <<term-system-component,system component>>.
--

[.testing]
.Testing procedures
[[TP-4.1.1.a]]4.1.1.a:: Examine the account management procedure to verify that it requires a unique user identifier for each person and each automated process.
[[TP-4.1.1.b]]4.1.1.b:: Examine the account list of a sample of system components to verify that no user identifier is assigned to more than one person.

[.guidance]
.Guidance
****
When several people share one identifier, actions cannot be traced to an individual. Service accounts used by software are in scope: each one needs its own identifier and a named owner.
****

[[REQ-4.1.2]]
[.requirement]
==== Removal of access on departure

[.statement]
--
The entity shall disable the user identifiers of a person within one business day of that person leaving the entity.
--

[.testing]
.Testing procedures
[[TP-4.1.2.a]]4.1.2.a:: Examine the leaver procedure to verify that it defines who disables user identifiers and within what time.
[[TP-4.1.2.b]]4.1.2.b:: Examine records for a sample of people who left in the last six months to verify that their user identifiers were disabled within one business day.

[.guidance]
.Guidance
****
Accounts that remain active after a person leaves are a common route for unauthorised access. Linking the leaver procedure to the human resources system reduces the delay.
****

[[access-review]]
=== Access review

[[REQ-4.2.1]]
[.requirement]
==== Periodic review of access rights

[.statement]
--
The entity shall review access rights at least as often as <<table-review-frequency>> specifies, and shall remove any access that is no longer needed.

The entity should record the outcome of each review, including the name of the reviewer.
--

[.testing]
.Testing procedures
[[TP-4.2.1.a]]4.2.1.a:: Examine review records to verify that reviews took place at the frequency that <<table-review-frequency>> specifies.
[[TP-4.2.1.b]]4.2.1.b:: Interview the people responsible to verify that access identified as no longer needed was removed.
[[TP-4.2.1.c]]4.2.1.c:: Examine a sample of <<term-privileged-account,privileged accounts>> to verify that each one has a documented business reason.

[.guidance]
.Guidance
****
Access tends to accumulate as people change roles. A review is most effective when the person who performs it understands what each role needs.
****
