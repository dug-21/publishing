== Implementation guidance (informative)

This annex gives additional information to help an entity apply the requirements. It contains no requirements.

[[annex-a-data-flow]]
=== Where encryption applies

<<figure-data-flow>> shows a typical flow of member data. Encryption in transit, described in <<REQ-5.2.1>>, applies to the link between the member and the entity. Encryption of stored data, described in <<REQ-5.1.1>>, applies to the data store.
