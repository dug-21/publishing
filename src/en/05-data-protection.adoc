[[data-protection]]
== Data protection

Member data is unreadable to anyone who obtains it without authorisation, whether it is stored or in transit.

[[storage]]
=== Storage

[[REQ-5.1.1]]
[.requirement]
==== Encryption of stored member data

[.statement]
--
The entity shall render stored <<term-member-data,member data>> unreadable by using <<term-strong-cryptography,strong cryptography>>.

The entity shall store cryptographic keys separately from the data that they protect.
--

[.testing]
.Testing procedures
[[TP-5.1.1.a]]5.1.1.a:: Examine system configurations to verify that stored member data is encrypted with strong cryptography.
[[TP-5.1.1.b]]5.1.1.b:: Examine key storage locations to verify that keys are not stored with the data that they protect.

[.guidance]
.Guidance
****
Encryption protects data only while the keys stay secret. A key stored beside the data that it protects gives little protection if the storage is copied.footnote:[Hardware security modules and managed key services are two common ways to separate keys from data.]
****

[[REQ-5.1.2]]
[.requirement]
==== Retention limits

[.statement]
--
The entity shall define a retention period for each category of member data, and shall securely delete member data when its retention period ends.
--

[.testing]
.Testing procedures
[[TP-5.1.2.a]]5.1.2.a:: Examine the retention policy to verify that it defines a retention period for each category of member data.
[[TP-5.1.2.b]]5.1.2.b:: Examine a sample of data stores to verify that they hold no member data older than the defined retention period.

[.guidance]
.Guidance
****
Data that has been deleted cannot be stolen. Automated deletion jobs are more reliable than manual clean-up.
****

[[transmission]]
=== Transmission

[[REQ-5.2.1]]
[.requirement]
==== Encryption in transit

[.statement]
--
The entity shall protect member data sent over any network outside its control by using Transport Layer Security version 1.3, as specified in <<ref-rfc8446,IETF RFC 8446>>, or a protocol of equivalent strength.

The entity may use an earlier protocol version on an internal network segment that carries no member data.
--

[.testing]
.Testing procedures
[[TP-5.2.1.a]]5.2.1.a:: Examine the configuration of a sample of external connections to verify that they accept only the protocol versions that the requirement allows.
[[TP-5.2.1.b]]5.2.1.b:: Observe a sample of transmissions to verify that member data is encrypted in transit.

[.guidance]
.Guidance
****
Networks outside the control of the entity include the internet, wireless networks and links operated by third parties. <<figure-data-flow>> shows where encryption applies.
****
