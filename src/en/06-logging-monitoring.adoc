[[logging-monitoring]]
== Logging and monitoring

Activity that affects member data is recorded, kept and reviewed so that misuse can be detected and investigated.

[[audit-logs]]
=== Audit logs

[[REQ-6.1.1]]
[.requirement]
==== Recording of access to member data

[.statement]
--
The entity shall record every access to member data in an audit log. Each log entry shall include:

* the <<term-user-identifier,user identifier>>;
* the date and time, in the format that <<ref-rfc3339,IETF RFC 3339>> specifies;
* the action performed;
* whether the action succeeded.
--

[.testing]
.Testing procedures
[[TP-6.1.1.a]]6.1.1.a:: Examine logging configurations to verify that access to member data is recorded.
[[TP-6.1.1.b]]6.1.1.b:: Examine a sample of log entries to verify that each one includes all the listed fields.

[.guidance]
.Guidance
****
A log that omits the user identifier cannot support an investigation. A single time format across all system components makes it possible to place events in order.
****

[[REQ-6.1.2]]
[.requirement]
==== Retention and review of audit logs

[.statement]
--
The entity shall keep audit logs for at least 12 months, and shall review them for unusual activity at least once each week.
--

[.testing]
.Testing procedures
[[TP-6.1.2.a]]6.1.2.a:: Examine log storage to verify that audit logs from the last 12 months are available.
[[TP-6.1.2.b]]6.1.2.b:: Examine review records to verify that reviews took place at least once each week.
[[TP-6.1.2.c]]6.1.2.c:: Interview the people responsible to verify that they follow up on unusual activity.

[.guidance]
.Guidance
****
Many incidents are discovered months after they begin. Automated alerting can support the weekly review but does not replace it.
****
